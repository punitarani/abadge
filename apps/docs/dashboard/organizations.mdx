Every resource in abadge belongs to an organization. The dashboard always operates against the **active organization**, shown in the header.

## Creating an organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the org switcher">
    Click the organization name in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Create organization">
    Enter a display name. The slug is auto-generated; override it if you want a specific URL fragment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land inside the new org">
    The dashboard switches context. The new org has no profiles, items, agents, or permissions yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching organizations

The org switcher lists every organization you're a member of. Selecting one updates the active context and reloads the items, agents, permissions, and audit views.

The active organization is also persisted to the Better Auth session, so subsequent dashboard visits land in the same place.

## Inviting members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Members">
    Available to `owner` and `admin` roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Choose a role: `owner`, `admin`, or `member`. The dashboard generates a one-time invite token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite link">
    Copy the link from the success dialog and send it through your usual channel. The link expires after 7 days; the token can be redeemed exactly once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recipient accepts">
    The recipient signs in (or creates an account) and lands at `/invite/accept?token=...`. After confirming the org name and role, they're added with the assigned role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The invite token is shown once. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored. If you lose the link, revoke the pending invitation and create a new one.
</Warning>

## Member roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Everything, including delete the organization |
| `admin` | Invite, remove, and update members; manage org settings |
| `member` | Read org resources; create and manage their own agents and items |

## Managing members

The members table on **Settings → Members** lets owners and admins:

- Change a member's role
- Remove a member (cascades agent revocation)
- Revoke a pending invitation

## Removing the organization

Deleting an organization cascades to its agents, permissions, profiles, and items. Audit entries persist (they have no foreign-key constraints). This is irreversible.
